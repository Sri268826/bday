Harshini's photos live here as 1.jpeg, 2.jpeg ... 10.jpeg (the number is the order on the site).

To add another photo: save it as the next number (11.jpeg) and add one more
caption line in the CONFIG section of index.html.

.jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp all work. iPhone .HEIC photos will NOT show - convert them to JPEG first.
